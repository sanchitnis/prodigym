## 🚀 For Students (Tech, Design & Education)

### 🌾 1. AgriSense: Edge-AI Crop Disease Diagnostic
**Domain**: Agriculture & Rural Development  
**Srujana Stage**: Stage 2 (Prototype to Product)  

**The Problem**: Smallholder farmers often misdiagnose crop diseases, leading to incorrect pesticide use. They lack reliable internet in the field to use cloud-based diagnostic apps.  
**The Solution**: An offline-first mobile web app that runs a lightweight ML model directly on the phone's browser to identify common leaf diseases.  

- **Mentor**: [Open - Seeking Tech Lead]
- **Current Team**: 2 Students (Need 2-3 more)
- **Skills Needed**: React/Next.js, TensorFlow.js, UX Design
- **Time Commitment**: 5-10 hours/week

### 🏥 2. MedAssist: Rural Healthcare Triage Assistant
**Domain**: Healthcare & Civic Tech  
**Srujana Stage**: Stage 1 (Foundational Research)  

**The Problem**: Frontline health workers (ASHA workers) in rural clinics are overwhelmed with patient intake and often miss early warning signs of complex conditions due to sheer volume.  
**The Solution**: An LLM-augmented voice-to-text assistant that listens to the patient intake conversation and flags potential high-risk symptoms for the human worker to review.  

- **Mentor**: Dr. Sanjay Chitnis
- **Current Team**: 1 Student (Need 3-4 more)
- **Skills Needed**: Python, OpenAI/Gemini API, Voice Processing, Prompt Engineering
- **Time Commitment**: 5-10 hours/week

### 📚 3. SrujanaBuddy: AI Peer Mentor
**Domain**: Education & Student Success  
**Srujana Stage**: Stage 2 (Prototyping & AI Integration)  

**The Problem**: Students often struggle to connect their daily learning with their long-term career aspirations, getting lost in generic advice.   
**The Solution**: An AI-powered peer mentor that understands a student's personal aspirations, recommends specific micro-challenges, and acts as a 24/7 co-pilot to help them take concrete steps towards their goals.  

- **Repo**: [sanchitnis/SrujanaBuddy](https://github.com/sanchitnis/SrujanaBuddy)
- **Mentor**: Dr. Sanjay Chitnis
- **Current Team**: Actively building (Looking for contributors)
- **Skills Needed**: Node.js/Python, LLM Prompt Engineering, RAG
- **Time Commitment**: 5-10 hours/week

- **Current Team**: 0 Students / 0 Faculty (Looking for co-builders)

- **Time Commitment**: 10+ hours/week
