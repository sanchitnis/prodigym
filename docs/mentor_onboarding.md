# Mentor Onboarding & Engagement Playbook

Welcome to Project PRODIGY. Mentorship is the cornerstone of our ecosystem. Unlike traditional, passive mentorship, PRODIGY mentors are active **Human-Experts-in-the-Loop (HEITL)** who validate, challenge, and guide AI-augmented student teams.

## 1. Mentor Tiers

*   **Tier A (Peer Mentors):** Advanced Stage 3/4 students guiding Stage 1/2 cohorts. Focus on day-to-day coding, prompt debugging, and sprint unblocking.
*   **Tier B (Faculty Mentors):** Professors guiding academic alignment, lab work, and methodological rigor.
*   **Tier C (Industry / R&D Mentors):** CTOs, Senior Engineers. Focus on enterprise architecture, TRL-based product development, and industry best practices.
*   **Tier D (Domain Experts):** Specialists (HealthTech, AgriTech, FinTech, Humanities). Focus on ensuring the solution actually solves a real-world problem (domain accuracy).
*   **Tier E (Strategic Advisors):** Venture builders, VCs, ecosystem leaders. Focus on Stage 4 venture incubation and scale.

## 2. Commitment Expectations

We respect your time. We ask for high-impact, low-frequency engagement:

*   **Asynchronous Review:** 1-2 hours a week reviewing PRs, architecture diagrams, or concept notes via GitHub or WhatsApp.
*   **Synchronous Touchpoints:** 30-minute bi-weekly or monthly sprint reviews with your assigned team via video call.
*   **"Monkey First" Focus:** Your primary job is to tell students when they are solving the *easy* problem instead of the *hard* problem (the Monkey). Don't let them build pedestals.

## 3. The HEITL Expert Jury Validation

For a project to advance from Stage 3 to Stage 4 (Venture Incubation), it must pass the HEITL Expert Jury Validation.

As a Tier C/D/E mentor, you may be called upon to serve on a Jury.

**Jury Responsibilities:**
1.  **Hallucination Check:** Ensure the AI-generated code/research is logically sound and factually correct.
2.  **Feasibility Check (SMI Score):** Evaluate the Solution Maturity Index. Can this actually be deployed in the target environment (e.g., low-bandwidth rural clinic)?
3.  **Value Check:** Does it solve a real "Job-to-be-Done" better than existing alternatives?

## 4. How to Get Started

1. Complete the [Registration Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf6CkQQXXr3306tPjUFi6nODpdLXT-F6uyFrLpoEcK5iBAa5Q/viewform).
2. You will be added to the Mentor WhatsApp group and the GitHub organization.
3. Review the `planning/task_board.md` or `data/projects.json` to find an active team that aligns with your expertise.
4. Introduce yourself in their project thread/issue.
