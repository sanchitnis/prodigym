# Points and Recognition System

PRODIGY gamifies the learning and contribution process. Points and recognition badges translate directly to our Leaderboards, demonstrating a participant's capability to mentors, industry partners, and peers.

## 1. The HEITL Mastery Levels

We define a participant's competency as a Human-Expert-in-the-Loop (HEITL) using the following progression:

*   **Level -2 (Observer):** Just observing, consuming content, no active participation.
*   **Level -1 (Novice):** Tries basic tasks but struggles. Over-relies on AI without checking, resulting in hallucinations or low-quality work.
*   **Level 0 (Baseline):** Can complete structured micro-tasks. Starting to use AI responsibly but needs significant peer guidance.
*   **Level 1 (Contributor):** Consistently delivers quality micro-tasks. Verifies AI output. Demonstrates basic 4 C skills. (Typically Stage 1 complete).
*   **Level 2 (Practitioner):** Handles complex, multi-step tasks. Effectively guides AI agents to produce robust artifacts. Good teamwork. (Typically Stage 2 complete).
*   **Level 3 (Specialist):** Leads small teams/projects. Demonstrates deep domain knowledge combined with expert AI orchestration. (Typically Stage 3 complete).
*   **Level 4 (Architect):** Designs complex systems or research probes. Mentors Level 1/2 participants. (Typically Stage 4).
*   **Level 5 (Master):** Enterprise-grade venture builder or deep tech innovator. Creates original IP.

## 2. The 4 Cs Evaluation Rubric

While technical tasks earn base points, multipliers are applied based on the demonstration of the 4 Cs:

*   **Critical Thinking:** Did the participant identify the *real* problem (the Monkey) rather than just the obvious symptoms? Did they catch AI hallucinations?
*   **Communication:** Is the documentation clear? Are PR descriptions well-written? Are they communicating effectively in standups/WhatsApp?
*   **Collaboration:** Are they helping peers? Are they building upon others' work rather than starting from scratch unnecessarily?
*   **Creativity:** Did they find an elegant, non-obvious solution to a bottleneck?

## 3. Earning Points

Points are currently tracked manually via Google Sheets and pushed to `data/leaderboard.json`.

*   **Micro-tasks (e.g., Bug bash, small doc edits):** 10 - 50 points.
*   **Medium Tasks (e.g., Implementing a UI component, writing a wiki article):** 100 - 300 points.
*   **Major Milestones (e.g., Completing a Stage 2 internship project):** 500 - 1000 points.
*   **Community Support:** Answering questions, reviewing PRs, or mentoring others earns continuous bonus points (e.g., 20 points per helpful review).

## 4. Badges & Rewards

Reaching specific point thresholds unlocks Badges (e.g., "SPOT-PROBE Specialist", "HEITL Master"). These badges are displayed on the global leaderboard and serve as verifiable credentials for industry partners.
