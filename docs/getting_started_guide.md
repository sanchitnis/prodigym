# COLLABORATOR GETTING STARTED GUIDE: PROJECT PRODIGY

> **Welcome to Project PRODIGY!** Whether you are a student, faculty member, mentor, institutional leader, or support expert, this guide outlines your exact 3-step onboarding path and how to make your first high-impact contribution.

---

### 🎓 1. For Students & Life-Long Learners (Faculty, Founders, Developers)

#### Goal: Build real-world AI + domain portfolio, master the 4 C skills, and progress from Stage 1 to Stage 4 venture creation.

1. **Step 1 — Register**: Complete the [Official Google Form Registration](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScPRODIGYEcosystemRegistration/viewform) managed by Dr. Sanjay Chitnis (`sanjay.chitnis@gmail.com`).
2. **Step 2 — Pick a Micro-Challenge (Stage 1)**: Browse active probes on the [Task Board](../planning/task_board.md) or open projects in [`data/projects.json`](../data/projects.json).
3. **Step 3 — Build & Submit**:
   - Use the [Gemini Prompt Playbook](../notebooks/gemini_prompt_playbook.md) and [Ramesh Raskar Spot-Probe Strategy](../wiki/spot_probe_strategy.md) to build a prototype.
   - Submit your contribution log entry in [`planning/standups_and_contributions.md`](../planning/standups_and_contributions.md).

---

### 👨‍🏫 2. For Mentors & HEITL Jury Members

#### Goal: Guide student-agent project teams, review Solution Maturity Index (SMI) scores, and co-author R&D IP.

1. **Step 1 — Register**: Register as a Mentor via the [Google Form Registration](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScPRODIGYEcosystemRegistration/viewform).
2. **Step 2 — Review Checklists**: Familiarize yourself with the [AI Review Checklists in AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).
3. **Step 3 — Conduct HEITL Reviews**: Audit student probe submissions in [`logs/agent_suggestions.md`](../logs/agent_suggestions.md) and assign SMI scores (Scale 1–10).

---

### 🏛️ 3. For Institutional Hubs (Universities, Colleges & R&D Labs)

#### Goal: Embed Srujana Pathway R&D into STEAM curricula, establish institutional clubs, and compete on the global leaderboard.

1. **Step 1 — Register Hub**: Register your institution via [Google Form Registration](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScPRODIGYEcosystemRegistration/viewform).
2. **Step 2 — Adopt Srujana Curriculum**: Integrate the 4 Developmental Stages ([references/srujana-pathway.md](../references/srujana-pathway.md)) into semester capstone projects or student clubs.
3. **Step 3 — Track Institutional Score**: Monitor your institution's contribution velocity on the live [Leaderboard](../index.html#leaderboard).

---

### 🛠️ 4. For Support Experts & Ecosystem Partners (IP, Infra, VCs)

#### Goal: Access pre-vetted Stage 3 & 4 venture deal-flow, provide patent/legal advisory, or sponsor compute resources.

1. **Step 1 — Partner Registration**: Complete the registration form selecting *Support Expert / Partner*.
2. **Step 2 — Review Stage 3/4 Ventures**: Audit high-SMI ventures ready for commercialization in [`data/projects.json`](../data/projects.json).
3. **Step 3 — Engage & License**: Partner on patent filings, IP licensing, or Stage-Gate seed capital deployment.
