## 3. Strategic Synthesis: The PRODIGY Meta-Venture Paradigm

By synthesizing learnings from these 12 global models, PRODIGY establishes a 4-pillar meta-strategy:

1. **Pre-Competitive Digital Public Goods (Inspired by iSPIRT & Amul)**:
   PRODIGY operates as a volunteer-augmented think tank, building open datasets, AI agent workflows, and software specifications as Digital Public Goods.
2. **Active Peer Learning & Challenge Sprints (Inspired by École 42, Minerva & Apple CBL)**:
   Students enter via zero-barrier micro-challenges, evaluated on Habits of Mind (HCAs) and the 4 Cs (Critical Thinking, Communication, Collaboration, Creativity) through peer correction.
3. **Brutal Technical De-risking (Inspired by Google X & MIT REDX)**:
   Teams apply the *Monkey-First Principle* during 1–2 week Spot-Probe micro-sprints to solve the hardest technical bottlenecks first.
4. **Stage-Gated Venture Incubation (Inspired by YC, EF & Aalborg PBL)**:
   Srujana Stage 3 & 4 projects transition into commercial software contracts, corporate IP licensing, or VC-backed startup spin-offs.
