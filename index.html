🌱 Mentoring Framework

The Srujana 4-Stage Pathway

A flexible developmental timeline integrating academic learning, industry exposure, deep R&D, and enterprise incubation.

STAGE 1 🎓

Foundational Skills & Readiness

Enhanced curriculum, student clubs, self-paced learning, and fundamental technical/AI capabilities for real-world internship readiness.

Mentoring: Self-Directed & Guided
STAGE 2 💼

Practical Exposure & Internships

Industry-mentored projects connecting learners with business partners to solve practical problems with professional workflows.

Mentoring: Industry Workflow Guided
STAGE 3 🧪

Product R&D & Deep Inquiry

Designing tangible products, consulting deliverables, applied research, and intellectual property (IP) creation with high SMI scores.

Mentoring: R&D Advisors & HEITL
STAGE 4 🚀

Enterprise Incubation & Elite Placement

Commercialization, startup incubation, executing consulting contracts, securing patents, and marquee AI-era career placement.

Mentoring: Business Incubation & VCs

The 4 Core Mentoring Philosophy Pillars

🛠️

I. Learn by Doing

Experiential execution over passive lecturing. Participants learn by building, testing, failing, and iterating with immediate feedback on active projects.

🤖

II. Augmented by AI

Utilizing AI tools as strategic force-multipliers for research, coding, and writing while maintaining prompt rigor, auditing, and academic integrity.

🤝

III. Human-Centric Skills

Placing a premium on critical thinking, ethics, emotional intelligence, leadership, collaboration, and communication (the 4 Cs) as technical tasks automate.

🏆

IV. AI-Era Outcomes

Tangible impact: Securing marquee AI-era careers, compiling documented code/patent portfolios, and building individual & institutional branding.
