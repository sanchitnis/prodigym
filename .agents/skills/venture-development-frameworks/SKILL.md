# Venture Development & Evaluation Frameworks

This skill provides a comprehensive operational toolkit for AI subagents, incubators, venture studios, and strategists to discover, de-risk, evaluate, and scale high-impact social and deep-tech ventures.

Frameworks for venture review, evaluation, and execution are categorized into four distinct operational phases:
1. **Opportunity Discovery & Deep-Tech Innovation**
2. **Hypothesis-Driven Validation & Product-Market Fit**
3. **User-Centric & Strategic Value Architecture**
4. **Stage-Gate & Capital Deployment Methodologies**

---

## 1. Opportunity Discovery & Deep-Tech Innovation

### Ramesh Raskar’s SPOT-PROBE (MIT Media Lab / REDX)
Designed to prevent "aiming the cannon in the wrong direction" by separating problem space analysis from solution execution. It avoids jumping into building before rigorously dissecting domain realities.

* **Core Sequence:** **Spot** (identify grand challenges, build problem canvases, map stakeholders/resources) $\rightarrow$ **Probe** (deconstruct root causes, run small low-cost technical/domain probes) $\rightarrow$ **Grow** (stakeholder adoption) $\rightarrow$ **Launch** (scale).
* **Key Mechanisms:**
  * *Brainswarming* over traditional brainstorming.
  * *Time-Cost-Reach* findings plots.
  * *Hexagon of Ideas* (evaluating multi-disciplinary fusion).
* **Best Used For:** Hard-tech, deep-tech, AI/hardware convergence, and social impact ventures tackling grand challenges where the exact problem definition is obscured by technical noise.
* **Reference:** [How to Think Like an MIT Media Lab Inventor: Ramesh Raskar at TEDxBeaconStreet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYnJPtEJj4s)

### Google X "Moonshot" Methodology
Evaluates frontier technology ventures through a brutal, fast-de-risking filter.

* **Core Criteria (3-Part Intersection):**
  1. A huge problem affecting millions/billions.
  2. A radical, sci-fi-sounding proposed solution.
  3. A breakthrough technology that makes the solution plausible within 5–10 years.
* **Key Mechanism:** *Kill-Fast Culture & Monkey-First Principle*. If a project requires training a monkey to recite Shakespeare while standing on a pedestal, you build the monkey's training first, not the pedestal. Focus immediately on the hardest technical bottleneck rather than low-hanging operational wins.
* **Best Used For:** Frontier ventures, venture studios, and breakthrough R&D labs.

## 2. Hypothesis-Driven Validation & Product-Market Fit

### Eric Ries’ Lean Startup Framework
Focuses on eliminating waste by managing uncertainty through rapid iteration and empirical validation.

* **Core Loops:** **Build $\rightarrow$ Measure $\rightarrow$ Learn** via Minimum Viable Products (MVPs).
* **Key Concepts:**
  * *Validated Learning:* Testing fundamental hypotheses (Value Hypothesis vs. Growth Hypothesis) via controlled experiments.
  * *Pivot vs. Persevere:* Structural review gates to decide whether to alter strategy or double down on current traction metrics.
* **Best Used For:** Software-driven ventures, consumer internet, and early-stage SaaS seeking initial traction under extreme market uncertainty.

### Steve Blank’s Customer Development Method
The predecessor and strategic backbone to Lean Startup, explicitly separating customer discovery from product execution.

* **Four-Step Model:** Customer Discovery $\rightarrow$ Customer Validation $\rightarrow$ Customer Creation $\rightarrow$ Company Building.
* **Core Principle:** "Get Out Of The Building" (GOOTB). Hypotheses are tested directly against user feedback to confirm customer-problem fit before writing substantial code or allocating capital.
* **Best Used For:** B2B, enterprise sales, and early-stage technology startups validating market demand prior to scaling sales ops.

## 3. User-Centric & Strategic Value Architecture

### Strategyzer (Osterwalder & Pigneur) — Business Model & Value Proposition Canvas
Visual frameworks used to map, test, and pivot core commercial logic.

* **Key Components:**
  * **Business Model Canvas (BMC):** Synthesizes 9 building blocks (Value Propositions, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Activities, Key Resources, Key Partners, Cost Structure).
  * **Value Proposition Canvas:** Zoomed-in alignment mapping **Customer Profile** (Jobs, Pains, Gains) directly against the **Value Map** (Products/Services, Pain Relievers, Gain Creators).
* **Best Used For:** Executive venture reviews, board-level strategic alignment, and auditing structural risk in existing business models.

### Clayton Christensen’s Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)
Focuses on consumer motivation rather than superficial demographics.

* **Core Premise:** Customers do not buy products; they "hire" products or services to perform a specific "job" and achieve progress in a specific context.
* **Key Mechanisms:** Uncovers functional, emotional, and social dimensions of consumption to identify underserved needs and true competitive alternatives (e.g., a morning milkshake competing with a bagel, a banana, or boredom during a commute).
* **Best Used For:** Product redesigns, market expansion, and discovering non-obvious market opportunities.

### Jake Knapp’s Design Sprint (GV / Google Ventures)
A compressed 5-day process to answer critical business questions through design, prototyping, and testing ideas with final users.

* **Sequence:** Day 1: Map $\rightarrow$ Day 2: Sketch $\rightarrow$ Day 3: Decide $\rightarrow$ Day 4: Prototype $\rightarrow$ Day 5: Test.
* **Best Used For:** Rapidly de-risking high-stakes feature sets, new product verticals, or UI/UX paradigms before committing engineering resources.

## 4. Stage-Gate & Capital Deployment Methodologies

### Disciplined Entrepreneurship (Bill Aulet / MIT)
A rigorous, systematic 24-step framework designed to take academic or technical research to market systematically.

* **Key Milestones:** Calculating Total Addressable Market (TAM) for the beachhead market, defining the Persona, mapping the Decision-Making Unit (DMU), calculating Customer Acquisition Cost (COCA) vs. Lifetime Value (LTV), and designing a scalable business model.
* **Best Used For:** University spin-outs, engineering founders, and structured accelerator programs.

### Stage-Gate System (Robert G. Cooper)
A formal management framework dividing the innovation process into distinct stages separated by evaluation gates.

* **Structure:** Idea Generation $\rightarrow$ Gate 1 (Scoping) $\rightarrow$ Stage 1 $\rightarrow$ Gate 2 (Business Case) $\rightarrow$ Stage 2 (Development) $\rightarrow$ Gate 3 (Testing) $\rightarrow$ Stage 3 (Launch).
* **Best Used For:** Hardware ventures, medical devices, deeptech, and corporate venture builders where capital commitments grow exponentially at each phase.

## 📊 Summary Comparison Matrix

| Methodology | Primary Goal | De-risking Vector | Ideal Venture Stage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SPOT-PROBE** | Problem formulation & solution probing | Misaligned problem selection / blindspots | Pre-Ideation / Research Lab |
| **Google X Moonshot** | Radical de-risking of breakthrough tech | Bottleneck technical feasibility | Frontier R&D / Studio |
| **Lean Startup** | Iterative product development | Market acceptance & adoption velocity | Early Stage / MVP |
| **Customer Dev** | Validating market & buyer persona | Commercial & sales model mismatch | Pre-Seed / Seed |
| **Design Sprint** | Rapid UX & feature prototyping | Usability & functional value alignment | Any / Feature Addition |
| **Jobs-to-be-Done** | Identifying context-driven consumer jobs | Surface-level demographic misalignment | Product Positioning |
| **Strategyzer Canvas**| Strategic business model mapping | Structural unit economics & partner risks | Executive Review / Pitch |
| **Disciplined Ent.** | End-to-end execution blueprint | Unit economics & market sizing bottlenecks | Seed / Early Commercialization |
| **Stage-Gate** | Governance & capital allocation | Financial & regulatory execution failure | Scale-Up / Hardware / Corporate |

## 🤖 Agent Execution Guide: Framework Selection Protocol

When evaluating or orchestrating a venture, agents should select the framework based on venture maturity and de-risking requirements:

1. **Pre-Ideation / Problem Definition Phase**: Activate **SPOT-PROBE** (to avoid building for false problems) or **Google X Moonshot** (if confronting deep technical bottlenecks using the *Monkey-First Principle*).
2. **Customer & Value Mapping Phase**: Apply **Customer Development (GOOTB)** alongside **Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)** to map true user motivations.
3. **Prototyping & UX De-risking Phase**: Run a **Design Sprint** (5-day rapid cycle) or **Lean Startup MVP** loop (*Build-Measure-Learn*).
4. **Business Model & Market Sizing Phase**: Synthesize using **Strategyzer Canvas** (BMC/VPC) and **Disciplined Entrepreneurship** (TAM, Persona, DMU, COCA vs. LTV).
5. **Capital Deployment & Scaling Phase**: Enforce **Stage-Gate** governance to validate criteria before committing capital or launching spin-offs.
