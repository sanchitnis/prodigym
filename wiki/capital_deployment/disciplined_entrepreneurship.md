# 🎓 Disciplined Entrepreneurship (Bill Aulet / MIT)

Developed by Bill Aulet at the MIT Sloan Center for Entrepreneurship, **Disciplined Entrepreneurship** is a rigorous 24-step framework designed to systematically take academic, engineering, or research breakthroughs to market.

---

## 🗺️ Key Milestones & Core Steps

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        DISCIPLINED ENTREPRENEURSHIP MILESTONES                         │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. MARKET SEGMENTATION ──> 2. BEACHHEAD TAM ──> 3. PERSONA DEFINITION                  │
│    (Identify 6-12 sectors)   (Calculate specific TAM)  (Detailed buyer profile)        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. COCA VS. LTV RATIO  ──> 5. DMU & SALES PROCESS ──> 6. SCALABLE BUSINESS MODEL       │
│    (LTV > 3x COCA)           (Decision-Making Unit)    (Unit economics validation)     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 🔑 Key Mechanisms

1. **Beachhead Market Selection**: Focus 100% of early energy on winning a single, highly specific market segment before expanding.
2. **Total Addressable Market (TAM) Calculation**: Calculate bottom-up TAM for the beachhead market (typically \$20M - \$100M/year).
3. **Decision-Making Unit (DMU)**: Map the Champion, Economic Buyer, and Veto Holders within customer organizations.
4. **COCA vs. LTV Ratio**: Ensure Lifetime Value (LTV) is at least **3x** the Customer Acquisition Cost (COCA), with COCA recovered within 12 months.
