# STRATEGY FRAMEWORK: RAMESH RASKAR'S SPOT-PROBE ENGINE FOR PRODIGY

> **Document Purpose**: A dedicated strategic methodology reference applying Professor Ramesh Raskar’s (MIT Media Lab) SPOT-PROBE Framework to guide problem identification, rapid AI-augmented prototyping, validation, and venture scaling for Project PRODIGY.

---

## 1. Executive Overview of the Spot-Probe Framework

Professor Ramesh Raskar’s innovation methodology centers on:
1. **Spotting**: Systematically identifying non-obvious, high-impact friction points and systemic bottlenecks across industries, institutions, and civic society.
2. **Probing**: Deploying low-cost, rapid, targeted micro-experiments ("probes") to test potential solutions in weeks rather than months, powered by AI augmentation.
3. **Pivoting / Validating**: Evaluating probe outputs using multi-tier review rubrics (automated screening $\rightarrow$ peer review $\rightarrow$ Human-Expert-in-the-Loop validation) to measure solution maturity.
4. **Scaling**: Incubating validated probes into commercial software products, enterprise consulting contracts, or standalone startup ventures.

### 2.1 Spot Phase: Problem Identification & Domain Mapping
Instead of starting with random startup ideas, PRODIGY teams systematically spot systemic inefficiencies:
* **Industry Bottlenecks**: Workflow friction, manual code refactoring, legacy infrastructure maintenance.
* **Higher Education Gaps**: Vanishing entry-level software/services roles for freshers, static academic curricula.
* **Civic & Societal Challenges**: AI governance, local hardware deployment, cross-disciplinary legal and architectural challenges.

### 2.2 Probe Phase: Rapid AI-Augmented Micro-Experiments
Probing tests hypotheses with minimal resource expenditure:
* Participants in Srujana Stage 1 & Stage 2 build micro-solutions using AI augmentation (autonomous agents, code generators, workflow automation).
* Micro-challenges issued by mentors test solution viability within short 1–2 week execution cycles.

### 2.3 Pivot / Validate Phase: Human-Expert-in-the-Loop (HEITL) Validation
Probes are evaluated through standardized rubrics:
* **Automated Screening**: Initial automated testing and code/eval checks.
* **Peer Review**: Peer-to-peer assessment enforcing collaboration and 4 C skills.
* **HEITL Expert Jury**: Subject-matter experts refine AI-generated outputs, ensuring technical rigor, ethical alignment, and legal compliance to establish the **Solution Maturity Index (SMI)**.

### 2.4 Scale Phase: Venture Hatching & Ecosystem Integration
Validated probes transition into commercial deployment:
* Srujana Stage 4 teams receive spin-off support, seed capital access, corporate IP transfer pathways, and marquee career placement.
