# 🎯 Apple Challenge-Based Learning (CBL) Framework

Developed by Apple Education in collaboration with exemplary educators, **Challenge-Based Learning (CBL)** is a multidisciplinary framework that encourages learners to leverage technology to solve authentic, real-world challenges in their local and global communities.

---

## 🔄 The CBL Operational Framework

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        CHALLENGE-BASED LEARNING (CBL) CYCLE                            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. BIG IDEA ──► 2. ESSENTIAL QUESTION ──► 3. THE CHALLENGE                             │
│    (Broad concept: Sustainability)  (Narrow focus: Water scarcity)  (Action: Reduce waste)│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. GUIDING QUESTIONS, ACTIVITIES & RESOURCES                                           │
│    (Sustained research, expert interviews, data gathering, technical spikes)           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. ACTIONABLE SOLUTIONS ──► 6. IMPLEMENTATION & EVAL ──► 7. PUBLISHING & REFLECTION   │
│    (Prototype creation)    (Deploy with live users)    (Public impact reporting)       │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 🛠️ Key Components of CBL

1. **Big Idea**: A broad topic of global or social importance (e.g., *Climate Resilience, Healthcare Access, Future of Work*).
2. **Essential Question**: A provocative question that contextualizes the Big Idea for the target community (e.g., *"How can primary health centers diagnose diseases faster with limited bandwidth?"*).
3. **The Challenge**: A concise, actionable call to action (e.g., *"Develop an offline-first AI diagnostic triage assistant for rural clinics"*).
4. **Guiding Questions & Resources**: A structured research phase where learners identify what they need to know, running technical spikes and stakeholder interviews.
5. **Implementation & Publishing**: Solutions are not left as classroom exercises; they are deployed in real-world contexts and published publicly.

---

## 💡 Application in Project PRODIGY

CBL provides the structural blueprint for **PRODIGY Micro-Challenges**:
- **Srujana Stage 1**: Challenges are framed around Big Ideas in agriculture, health, and education.
- **Authentic Publishing**: Every validated probe is published on the PRODIGY GitHub Pages portal and open-source R&D repository.
