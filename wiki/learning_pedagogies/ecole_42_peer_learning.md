# 🏊‍♂️ École 42 Model: Teacherless, Peer-to-Peer & Gamified Learning

Founded in Paris by Xavier Niel and Nicolas Sadirac, **École 42** is an innovative higher education model designed for computer science and software engineering. It operates with **zero teachers, zero lectures, zero textbooks, and zero tuition**, relying entirely on peer-to-peer learning, project-based progression, and gamification.

## 🛠️ Key Mechanisms

### 1. Peer Correction & Evaluation Credits
- Students earn **Correction Points** by reviewing peers' projects.
- To submit a project for grading, a student must spend Correction Points to purchase reviews from peers.
- This creates a self-sustaining economy of mutual feedback and forces students to master both **code authorship** and **code auditing**.

### 2. The Piscine ("The Pool") Selection Filter
- Candidates enter an intensive 4-week immersion ("The Piscine") working 12–15 hours/day on continuous coding challenges.
- Tests resilience, adaptability, and willingness to ask peers for help when stuck.

### 3. Teacherless Autonomy
- Without professors to give answers, students learn to consult documentation, test hypotheses, collaborate with peers, and debug independently.

## 💡 Application in Project PRODIGY

Project PRODIGY adapts the École 42 model into the **Srujana Stage -1 & 0 Pathway**:
- **Micro-Challenge Piscine**: Open-access 2-week micro-sprints where participants learn AI tooling via peer collaboration.
- **Peer Review Rubrics**: Peer-to-peer grading of micro-probes before submission to the HEITL Expert Jury.
- **Gamified Leaderboard**: Experience points (XP) and badges awarded for peer reviews, code contributions, and 4 C skill demonstrations.
