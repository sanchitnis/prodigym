# 🚪 Steve Blank's Customer Development Method

The **Customer Development Method**, formulated by Steve Blank, is the strategic foundation of modern startup validation. It explicitly separates customer discovery from product execution, preventing teams from scaling sales and marketing before verifying buyer demand.

---

## 🗺️ The Four-Step Customer Development Model

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          THE CUSTOMER DEVELOPMENT PROCESS                              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ PHASE 1: SEARCH (Iterative)                                                            │
│ 1. Customer Discovery  ──>  2. Customer Validation  ──>  [PIVOT GATE]                  │
│    (Test Problem/Solution)     (Test Business Model)                                   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ PHASE 2: EXECUTION                                                                     │
│ 3. Customer Creation   ──>  4. Company Building                                        │
│    (Scale End-User Demand)     (Transition from Startup to Enterprise Operations)       │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 🏃‍♂️ Core Principle: GOOTB ("Get Out Of The Building")

> *"There are no facts inside the building, only opinions."*

- **Hypothesis Testing**: Every assumption regarding target beneficiaries, pricing tolerance, and workflow pain points must be tested directly against real stakeholders.
- **Problem-First Discovery**: Interview users to understand their lived realities before attempting to pitch a solution.
