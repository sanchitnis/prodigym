# 🚀 Google X "Moonshot" Methodology & Monkey-First Principle

The **Google X Moonshot Methodology** is a framework for evaluating and brutal de-risking of frontier technology ventures. It forces teams to confront the hardest technical bottlenecks immediately rather than building easy operational features.

---

## 🎯 The 3-Part Moonshot Intersection

A project qualifies as a "Moonshot" only when it sits at the exact intersection of three criteria:

```
                  ┌───────────────────────────────┐
                  │ 1. A Huge Problem             │
                  │    Affecting millions/billions │
                  └──────────────┬────────────────┘
                                 │
                 ┌───────────────┴───────────────┐
                 │                               │
┌────────────────┴───────────────┐ ┌─────────────┴────────────────┐
│ 2. Radical Proposed Solution   │ │ 3. Breakthrough Technology    │
│    Sci-fi sounding concept     │ │    Plausible within 5–10 years │
└────────────────────────────────┘ └───────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 🐒 The "Monkey-First" Principle

> *"If you want to teach a monkey to recite Shakespeare while standing on a 10-foot pedestal, where should you start?"*

- **The Pedestal Fallacy**: Most teams build the pedestal first because it is easy, visual, and gives a false sense of progress.
- **The Monkey-First Discipline**: You must start by training the monkey. Focus **100% of initial R&D** on solving the hardest technical bottleneck. If you cannot train the monkey, building the pedestal is wasted effort.

```
❌ WRONG WAY: Build Pedestal First ──> Celebrate False Progress ──> Fail at Monkey Training
✅ MOONSHOT WAY: Tackle Hardest Bottleneck First (Train Monkey) ──> De-risk or Kill Fast
```

---

## 💥 Kill-Fast Culture

- Establish explicit **Kill Criteria** before launching a probe.
- Celebrate teams that fail fast and kill non-viable projects early, freeing up compute and talent for higher-yielding ventures.

---

## 💡 Application in Project PRODIGY

In **PRODIGY Stage 1 & 2**, student-agent teams identify the "Monkey" (e.g., offline AI inference speed on low-cost hardware) and test it first before designing complex UI dashboards or marketing pedestals.
