# 🇮🇳 iSPIRT Foundation & Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) Model

**iSPIRT Foundation** (Indian Software Product Industry Round Table) is a non-profit, volunteer-led product think tank that architected India's **Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)**—commonly known as the **India Stack** (including Aadhaar, UPI, Account Aggregator, ABDM/Health Stack, ONDC, and Beckn Protocol).

---

## 👥 Roles & Collaboration Protocol

iSPIRT's collaborative ecosystem operates through clear volunteer roles:

- **Product Architects**: Design unbundled open specifications and API schemas.
- **Evangelists & Community Leads**: Onboard developers, startups, and enterprise integrators.
- **Policy & Regulatory Leads**: Align open technical specs with national legal & data governance frameworks.
- **Volunteers for Public Good**: Engineers and researchers contributing time without monetary compensation to solve hard societal bottlenecks.

---

## 💡 Application in Project PRODIGY

PRODIGY adopts key iSPIRT principles for its **Meta-Venture Studio**:
- **Pre-Competitive R&D**: PRODIGY builds open datasets, AI agent workflows, and software specifications as Digital Public Goods.
- **Volunteer Mentor Network**: Senior engineers and researchers serve as pro-bono mentors in the HEITL Expert Jury.
- **Unbundled Venture Hatching**: Downstream student teams build commercial startups (Stage 4) on top of PRODIGY's open R&D foundation.
